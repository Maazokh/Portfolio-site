======================================================
HOW TO USE VIDEO TRANSITIONS IN YOUR PORTFOLIO
======================================================

1. FILE NAMES & LOCATION:
   Place your 0.25s - 0.3s transition video directly in this folder:
   
   - Name it: "transition.mp4" (or "transition.webm")
   
2. AUTOMATIC REVERSE:
   The website is already programmed to automatically detect the 
   direction you are hovering.
   
   - Moving Down (Button 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4):
     Plays "transition.mp4" forward (Left to Right dash).
     
   - Moving Back Up / Hovering back to the First Button:
     Automatically flips the video horizontally (Right to Left dash) 
     using hardware-accelerated CSS scaleX(-1)!
     
   - (Optional) If you have a separately rendered reverse video, 
     you can also name it "transition-rev.mp4" and it will use that!

3. RECOMMENDED VIDEO SPECS:
   - Length: 0.25 to 0.35 seconds (approx. 8 to 12 frames at 30-60fps)
   - Background: Transparent (WebM) or pure White #FFFFFF (MP4/WebM)
   - Audio: None / Muted (browsers require muted videos for hover autoplay)
   - Dimensions: 1920x1080 (16:9) or match your character canvas

Until you add your video, the website uses a built-in high-speed 
anime action-dash speed smear that already demonstrates the forward 
and reverse transitions!
